# Frozen-policy FP32 results

Eight RTX PRO 6000 GPUs; medians of five fresh-process runs. Bytes are total AllGather output / ReduceScatter input per rank. Each run is the slowest-rank mean of 20 queued calls. NCCL emits initialization logs only during these primary measurements. The policy was selected from separate calibration; no validation samples select configurations.

Both operations win all 21 sizes in all five rounds, and every observed UCCL range lies below the corresponding NCCL range. Full samples and chosen configurations are in the CSV files.
